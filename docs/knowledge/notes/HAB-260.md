# HAB-260: FakeRemoteConfigService replaces rather than layers RC overrides — needs a harness-defaults merge

## Debrief summary

### 2026-09-02

**What went well**
- The map-merge design (`FakeRemoteConfigService.withTestDefaults` layering test defaults under caller overrides at construction) was well received — clean, minimal-touch fix to a real recurring footgun.
- Low user involvement needed — plan approved once, then WU1→WU3 executed and shipped with review/CI passing each time, no rework cycles.

**What was hard or surprising**
- During WU2, the implementation subagent declined to merge its own PR even after being told the user had already approved it — it only accepted approval received directly in its own thread, not a relayed quote from the orchestrating session. Reasonable safeguard, but meant the orchestrator had to perform the `ship`/merge step itself instead.

**What to change**
- Documented the subagent merge-authority boundary in `docs/workflows/MULTI_WU.md` — orchestrator handles `ship` directly, in the main session, whenever a WU subagent won't act on a relayed approval.
