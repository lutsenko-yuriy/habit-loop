# HAB-263: Todo list: fill in-progress showups amber instead of the outline circle

## Notes

### 2026-09-02

**Nested ternaries reduce readability**

While writing the in-progress icon/color selection logic for the todo-list tile
(`isOnBreak ? a : isInProgress ? b : c`-shaped chains), the nested `? :` chain was
harder to scan than a flat conditional would be. Worth a general CODE_STYLE.md
guideline to prefer switch expressions or an extracted helper function over
stacking ternaries beyond one level.
