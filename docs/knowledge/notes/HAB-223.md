## Notes

### 2026-08-16

**Plan scoped down to WU1 only; WU2 (CI self-heal push to main) dropped**

The approved plan originally had two WUs: WU1 (`ship`-time rebase+regenerate, closes the merge-race window in the normal path) and WU2 (`index.py --heal` + a `heal-notes-index` CI job pushing directly to `main` with `[skip ci]`, mirroring `version-tag`'s existing build-number-bump pattern). User declined WU2 specifically because it pushes to `main` without a PR, and raised a broader question: should CI ever push directly to `main` at all — including `version-tag`'s existing build-number bump, not just this new job? That's out of scope for HAB-223 (a load-bearing, documented part of the release pipeline, not local to this ticket) — captured as a new ticket instead of folded in here. HAB-223 proceeds as WU1 only.
