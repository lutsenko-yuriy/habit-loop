## Debrief summary

### 2026-07-28

**What went well**
- The root cause was found and fixed quickly.

**What was hard or surprising**
- Landing on a state shape everyone was happy with took three iterations: `activeBreak` (unresolved break, for gating) plus a separate `isBreakActiveNow` bool; then a computed getter backed by a stored `now` timestamp (rejected — a raw `DateTime` leaking into UI state is worse than a bool); then a nullable `currentBreak: PactBreak?` field (rejected — two nullable/near-duplicate break references felt worse than one break plus one bool). Settled back on the original `activeBreak` + `isBreakActiveNow` bool, but that still means two properties have to independently agree to express one concept ("is a break active right now").

**What to change**
- Flagged for the next periodic checkup: revisit state shapes across the app for unnecessary degrees of freedom — cases where multiple fields can independently vary but only some combinations are ever valid (this `PactDetailState` pair is the concrete example that surfaced it). Not filed as its own ticket — intentionally left for the checkup's own dimension walk rather than pre-judged here.
